# First week

The goal for the week: understand how AI is built here well enough to explain the paved
roads to someone else, and ship one small change through the full review path.

## Day 2 — the paved roads

Read [Paved roads](../paved-roads/README.md) end to end. Note which road your team's
work sits on. If it sits on none, that is a conversation to have with your lead this week:
off-road builds need an architecture review before any code.

## Day 3 — first tutorial, first evaluation

- Complete [Tutorial 1: your first model call](../tutorials/01-first-call.md).
- Complete [Tutorial 5: evaluations](../tutorials/05-evaluations.md). You will be asked to
  write an evaluation before you are asked to write a prompt; that order is deliberate.

## Day 4 — security and responsible AI

- [Security for AI systems](../best-practices/security.md) — prompt injection, tool
  permissions, output handling.
- [Responsible AI](../best-practices/responsible-ai.md) — fairness, explainability, and
  when a human must be in the loop.

## Day 5 — meet the owners

Book 20 minutes with the owner of each area you will touch. Owners are listed in the
frontmatter of every page and in `kb.config.yaml`. Ask each one: "What do people get
wrong in their first month?" Write the answers into the [FAQ](../wiki/faq.md) as a proposed
change. That is your first contribution.

## Exit criteria

Tick the "week one" items on the [checklist](checklist.md).
