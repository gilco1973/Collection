# Reading list

Ordered. Each entry says why it matters *here*; skip nothing in the first block.

## Block 1 — required in week one

1. [Governance overview](../governance/README.md) — who decides what, and how a use case is approved.
2. [Data classification](../best-practices/data-classification.md) — the taxonomy every
   prompt and dataset is labelled with.
3. [Paved roads](../paved-roads/README.md) — the approved architectures.
4. [Security for AI systems](../best-practices/security.md) — threat model for LLM applications.
5. [Evaluation pipeline](../paved-roads/evaluation-pipeline.md) — how "it works" is proven.

## Block 2 — required in month one

6. [Prompt engineering](../best-practices/prompt-engineering.md).
7. [Agent design](../best-practices/agent-design.md) — when an agent is warranted and how
   its tools are permissioned.
8. [Model risk management](../governance/model-risk.md) — the supervisory expectation
   that shapes our lifecycle.
9. [Observability](../best-practices/observability.md) and [cost management](../best-practices/cost-management.md).
10. Decision records: [ADR-0001](../wiki/decision-records/ADR-0001-llm-gateway.md) and
    [ADR-0002](../wiki/decision-records/ADR-0002-human-in-the-loop.md).

## Block 3 — external references

Curated in [Resources](../resources/README.md). Start with the vendor documentation for
the model provider, the NIST AI Risk Management Framework, and the OWASP Top 10 for LLM
applications. External links are reviewed by the librarian; report broken ones.

## Videos

The [videos](../videos/README.md) section pairs each block above with recorded internal
sessions. Watch them after, not instead of, the reading.
