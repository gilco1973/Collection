# 30 / 60 / 90 days

These milestones describe what a productive contributor looks like. They are a shared
expectation between you and your manager, not a test.

## By day 30 — safe and oriented

- You can explain the three day-one rules and where each is enforced.
- You have shipped at least one change on a paved road with an evaluation attached.
- You have used the [LLM gateway](../paved-roads/llm-gateway.md) and can read its logs
  for your own requests.
- You know the model owner for every model your team uses.

## By day 60 — independent on the paved roads

- You have led one code review of AI-generated or AI-adjacent code using the
  [code review skill](../skills/code-review/SKILL.md).
- You have written or improved an evaluation set and can show its trend on the dashboard.
- You have raised at least one finding in the knowledge base (a broken page, a stale
  page, a missing answer) through the review process.

## By day 90 — improving the roads

- You have contributed a page, a skill or a tutorial that another team uses.
- You can run a [model risk](../governance/model-risk.md) pre-assessment for a new use
  case and know when to escalate.
- You have paired with someone newer than you on their first week.

## If you are a manager

Use the [checklist](checklist.md) at each milestone. Where an item is blocked by access,
tooling or missing documentation, file it against the knowledge base: the point of the
librarian is to make the next person's path shorter than yours.
