# 2. The top bar and the footer

*The same on every page.*

![The top bar on Discover: the four areas, search, the bell, your initials; the footer's sentence at the bottom of every page.](../attachments/02-the-top-bar-and-the-footer/02-the-top-bar-and-the-footer-01.jpg)

*The top bar on Discover: the four areas, search, the bell, your initials; the footer's sentence at the bottom of every page.*

| You see | It means |
| --- | --- |
| `Discover` · `My workspace` · `Build` · `Learn` | The four areas. Discover is the catalog. My workspace is what is yours. Build is where you propose and where components get signed. Learn is the roads, the collection and the programme. |
| `Search assistants, tools, knowledge…` `⌘K` | Opens the search box from anywhere. Ctrl+K on Windows. Section 13 explains what it finds. |
| The bell | Takes you to your workspace, where your requests and their state are. |
| Your initials | The account menu: `Settings` and `Sign out`. |
| `Everything in the Hub runs on the platform: one identity chain, one policy bundle, one audit trail.` | The footer's one sentence. Whatever you do here is done as you, checked by the same policy, and recorded. |
| `Office hour Thursdays 11:00 · escalation: platform lead` | Where to go with a question a page does not answer. |
